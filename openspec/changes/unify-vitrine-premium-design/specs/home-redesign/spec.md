# Spec: Home Redesign

## MODIFIED Requirements

#### Component: Vitrine Hero Section

- The home page MUST feature a "Hero" section that sets a professional and modern tone.
- The hero MUST include a subtle background visual (gradient or pattern) using the **Modern Blue** primary color.
- Typography MUST use the **Inter** font family with bold weight for headings.

#### Component: Search & Filter Hub

- The location and segment filters MUST be grouped in a single visual "Hub".
- The hub MUST use a semi-transparent background (Glassmorphism) with a `backdrop-blur` effect.
- The layout MUST be centered and responsive.

#### Component: Agency Card

- The store cards in the grid MUST show essential information (Name, Segment) without requiring a hover state.
- Information overlays MUST use a glassmorphism style (semi-transparent white or dark background depending on mode).
- Hovering over a card MUST trigger a smooth "Lift" effect (scaling + enhanced shadow).

#### Component: Category Cards

- Categories at the bottom MUST be displayed as distinct cards with:
  - Bold headings.
  - Counter of establishments.
  - Hover effects that change the border/shadow color to Primary Blue.
